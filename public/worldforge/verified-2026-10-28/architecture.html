BOUNDARY A

Fiction is not evidence for reality.

The fictional universe can inspire the system without being treated as proof of real-world claims. Separate in-world truth from external fact.

BOUNDARY B

Infrastructure does not dictate creativity.

The system supports creative decisions with memory and traceability. It should not force every narrative choice into a rigid template.
