STATUS / PROVENANCE / CONTINUITY

Governance & Integrity

The operational rules below are designed to protect original content, preserve unresolved contradictions, and avoid unsupported claims.

01

Established canon

Preserve established records and their status. Do not rewrite them merely to make the narrative simpler.

02

Unresolved

Keep contradictions and missing evidence visible. Do not invent a resolution or claim that a gap is closed.

03

Proposal

Newly generated material remains explicitly labeled as a proposal until authorized.

04

Provenance

Distinguish source text, interpretation, inference, and speculation. Record unknown sources as unknown.

CANON SAFEGUARDS

Project boundaries to preserve

  • Three Nines: Being, Becoming, Understanding are related perspectives, not compulsory stages.
  • AION / THE ONE: the encompassing metaphysical ground beneath the Three Nines, not simply another numbered level.
  • Level 120: philosophical and visual uses remain distinct until formally resolved.
  • Prompt Bible: the exact original full-text source remains unrecovered; a current methodology record is not a replacement.
  • Chronology vs genealogy: distinct relationships; do not treat one as proof of the other.
  • Archive gaps: missing documents and intermediate history remain visible as recovery targets.
