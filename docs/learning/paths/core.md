# AH-AIOS Core Learning Path

## Purpose

Develop understanding of AH-AIOS foundations and the governance and assurance concepts that bound system operation.

## Modules

1. **Core orientation** — purpose, system map, subsystem boundaries, terminology, and operating principles.
2. **Architecture foundations** — components, interfaces, dependencies, and separation of concerns.
3. **Identity and access** — identities, roles, authorization concepts, least privilege, and access lifecycle.
4. **Security and policy** — security boundaries, policy enforcement, threat awareness, and safe operation.
5. **Audit and evidence** — event records, provenance, evidence quality, retention concepts, and verification.
6. **Governance and controlled change** — approvals, version history, protected assets, change review, and rollback principles.

## Applied outcomes

Learner can explain the Core's responsibility, map a simple request through identity and policy checks, identify an evidence requirement, and describe how a governed change is recorded.

## Practice

- Draw a subsystem boundary map.
- Review a sample access-control scenario.
- Trace a sample change from proposal through review to recorded outcome.
- Identify what is demonstrated, what is assumed, and what remains unverified.

## Assessment evidence

Architecture explanation, access-control scenario analysis, and a change-governance walkthrough with explicit evidence references.

## Boundary

Learning materials explain intended architecture and documented implementation status. They do not assert that every target control is deployed or production-assured.
