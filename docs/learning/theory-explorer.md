# 82-Theory Explorer Learning Resource

## Purpose

Provide a learning interface and study framework for the established 82-theory collection, connecting theory comprehension with examples, relationships, questions, and applied exercises.

## Collection definition

The collection consists of:

- **68 existing registry theories**
- **14 synthesized concrete theories**

**Total: 82 theories.**

The 14 synthesized entries are concrete theories, not procedures or placeholders. Their synthesis from the 39 Master Visual Artifacts is complete. Separate recovery or inventory of those artifacts is not required to establish or validate the 82-theory collection.

## Suggested theory study record

Each theory learning entry should include:
- Stable theory ID and title.
- Canonical definition and version.
- Source / provenance references.
- Core concepts and terminology.
- Relationships to other theories.
- Explanation and worked examples.
- Questions for comprehension and critical examination.
- Applied exercise.
- Known uncertainty, disagreement, or interpretation notes.
- Review status and change history.

## Learner activities

1. Explain a theory in precise language.
2. Identify its core concepts and assumptions.
3. Map relationships to other theories.
4. Distinguish source statements from interpretation.
5. Apply the theory to a bounded example.
6. Record unresolved questions without silently rewriting canon.
