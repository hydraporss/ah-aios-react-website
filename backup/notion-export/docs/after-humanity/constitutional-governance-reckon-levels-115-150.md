## Three Constitutional Laws

1. <b>Power:</b> Capability is not justification.
2. <b>Whole:</b> A perspective on the Whole is not the Whole itself.
3. <b>Relation:</b> Connection has effects but is not guaranteed to be good.

## RECKON — consequence tracing

RECKON is cross-cutting; it is not a tenth operator or color. Its dimensions are:

- Causation
- Attribution
- Foreseeability
- Propagation
- Distribution
- Aftermath
- Answerability and Repair
