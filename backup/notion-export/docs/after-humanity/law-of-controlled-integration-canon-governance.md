## Canon status vocabulary

- <b>ESTABLISHED CANON</b> — adopted framework, rules, and definitions.
- <b>CANON-ALIGNED INTERPRETATION</b> — consistent interpretation; not itself a canon amendment.
- <b>APPROVED VISUALIZATION</b> — authorized visual reference; does not independently rewrite ontology.
- <b>VERIFY / UNDER REVIEW</b> — unresolved or evidence-pending.
- <b>NOT CANON</b> — excluded, speculative, or unapproved material.

Operational labels such as Draft, Proposed, Validated, or Approved must not be casually treated as equivalent to the canon-status vocabulary. Their meaning depends on the governing workflow.

## Controlled integration requirements

1. Preserve source lineage and the distinction between source and interpretation.
2. Record evidence and verification separately from claims of canon status.
3. Require deliberate authorization before canon changes.
4. Preserve version history; do not silently overwrite an approved state.
5. Keep applications, stories, generated images, and implementations from becoming canon by implication.
